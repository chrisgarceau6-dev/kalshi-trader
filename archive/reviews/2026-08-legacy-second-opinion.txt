Ran a backtest grouping Kalshi hourly WTI oil markets by close time. For each close time, collected all qualifying strikes (ask in [90,93]c, 150-600s remaining, prior 2 candles >=75c), sorted by ask descending (highest = most certain = "1st strike"). Results over 30 days, 33% OOS split:

- 2nd strike OOS: 32 trades, 93.8% WR, +$0.77/trade

KXWTIH 2nd strike met all four criteria. Question: Are these criteria appropriately tight or too loose for a 32-sample OOS window?

3. Survivorship bias fix in KXBTCD backtest

KXBTCD has ~188 strike prices per hourly close. Initial backtest scanned strikes $300-$2,500 below the close price, found 100% win rate. My assistant identified this as survivorship bias: scanning below the close price guarantees all selected strikes already resolved YES. Fix: use BTC's price at T-300s (trigger time, before close is known) as the scan reference, only use close price for settlement determination. Results after fix: 5% trigger rate, OOS n=6.

Question: Is the survivorship bias identification and fix correct? Is there any remaining bias in the corrected methodology?
